Plan questions

Do unused items really roll over?
Yes. Item counts you don't use in a plan period carry into the next period, so a quiet month doesn't cost you. Allowances do not roll over indefinitely — our team will confirm the current carry-over period when your plan is activated.
Is pickup and delivery included?
Every plan includes free pickup and delivery for the laundry requests it covers (2 to 4 a month, depending on the plan). Requests beyond that may attract a pickup fee, which we confirm before collecting.
What counts as a clothing item?
Each garment counts as one item, and each plan only covers the categories listed on its card. Items outside your plan’s categories are charged at the price-list rate.
Can I move to another plan later?
Yes. You can move up or down at renewal. Our team will confirm the effective date so your allowance is adjusted fairly.
Are dry cleaning and express service included?
Dry-cleaning counts are included from Standard upward, and express / semi-express uses are included from Standard upward, as shown on each plan. Anything beyond your plan allowance is charged at the published price-list rates, and we confirm before proceeding.

Prefer to pay as you go?

No plan needed. Book a pickup and pay per item at our published rates.

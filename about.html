About us

A local team that takes the work seriously

DIEMEZ CLEANS provides laundry, cleaning, painting and interior finishing services designed to make everyday life and business operations easier.

Who we are

Based in Agudama, close to Law School, in Bayelsa State, we deliver convenient service, careful workmanship and a dependable customer experience for households, businesses and institutions.

Our work covers three areas. Laundry and garment care — everyday washing, wash and fold, ironing, dry cleaning and household fabrics. Cleaning — for homes, offices, institutions and commercial premises. And painting and interior finishing, handled as scoped projects rather than one-size-fits-all jobs.

We keep our prices published, confirm appointments in advance, and stay reachable while your order is with us. If something isn't right, we want to hear about it so we can put it right.

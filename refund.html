Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: —

Cancelling or rescheduling a pickup

  • Contact us as early as you can with your booking reference, by phone 0812 951 1794 or on WhatsApp.
  • Cancellations made before our team is dispatched are free of charge.
  • If our team has already arrived or is on the way, a pickup fee may apply to cover the trip.
  • Rescheduling is subject to available slots. Repeated last-minute changes may affect future bookings.

Cancelling cleaning or painting work

  • Cancel more than 24 hours before the agreed start and no charge applies.
  • Cancel within 24 hours of the agreed start, and a fee reflecting mobilisation may apply.
  • Once work has begun, charges are based on the work completed and materials already used.

Refunds on laundry orders

  • If an order has not yet been processed, we can cancel it and refund any amount already paid.
  • Once cleaning has started, the order can no longer be cancelled, but we will review any quality concern with you.
  • Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible, within a reasonable period after approval.

Quality concerns

  • Tell us within 24 hours of delivery, with your booking reference and photos where possible.
  • Where a garment has not been cleaned properly, we will re-clean it at no charge as a first step.
  • If re-cleaning is not possible or does not resolve the issue, we will agree an appropriate refund for that item.

Damage or loss

  • Report it as soon as you notice it, and no later than 24 hours after delivery.
  • We will review the item, its care label and the service performed with you.
  • Where we are responsible, compensation is limited to the reasonable value of the item or the service charge for it.
  • We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, faults in the fabric, or damage arising from instructions on the care label being followed.

Monthly plans

  • Plans are billed monthly and can be paused or cancelled with notice before the next billing date.
  • Used item allowances are not refundable. Unused allowances roll over as described on your plan.
  • If we cancel a plan, the unused portion of the current period is refunded on a pro-rata basis.

How to raise a request

Call or WhatsApp 0812 951 1794, or email info.diemezcleans@gmail.com with your name, booking reference and a short description of the issue. We respond during business hours, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Everything about booking, pickup, turnaround and payment. If your question isn't here, message us on WhatsApp and we'll answer directly.

How do I book a service?
Head to the booking page, enter the items you're sending, choose a pickup date and time slot, and add your address. You'll get a booking reference straight away and our team will confirm the appointment. For painting, commercial cleaning or bulk laundry, use the quote request form instead.
Do you offer home pickup and delivery?
Yes. We collect from your home, office or business premises and return your items once they're ready. Pickup and delivery is included for standard laundry bookings and for monthly plan customers.
Which areas do you cover?
We're based in Agudama, close to Law School, and serve customers across Bayelsa State. If your address is outside our usual pickup areas, our team will let you know whether collection is possible before confirming.
How long does laundry take?
Standard laundry is processed and returned on our normal cycle; the confirmed turnaround is given when the order is accepted. Express service is available for eligible items, subject to capacity and a quoted express rate.
Do you offer express services?
Yes, for eligible items. Express depends on our capacity on the day, so it is confirmed at booking rather than guaranteed in advance. The express rate is quoted when you book — message us and we will confirm it before we start.
How are laundry prices calculated?
Per item, by service level — wash only, iron only, wash & iron, dry cleaning. Bulk laundry is charged by weight (₦1,200 per kg) or per cycle (₦15,000, up to 25 kg). Bedsheets, duvets and other household items are priced individually. Your estimated total appears before you submit a booking, and the final charge is confirmed when items are counted or weighed at pickup.
Which payment methods are accepted?
You can pay our team at pickup, or by bank transfer — account details are sent with your order confirmation. Monthly plans are invoiced separately. We'll confirm the current accepted methods when your booking is confirmed.
Can I reschedule a pickup?
Yes, with notice. Contact us by phone or WhatsApp with your booking reference and we'll move the pickup to another available slot. Rescheduling requests made after our team is already on the way may not be possible on the same day.
How do I request a painting or commercial cleaning quotation?
Use the quote request form and describe the space, the work required and the location. We may arrange a short visit to confirm the scope, then send you a written price before anything begins.
How can I contact customer support?
Call 0812 951 1794 or message us on WhatsApp during business hours, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. You can also email info.diemezcleans@gmail.com or use the contact form. Enquiries received outside those hours are answered the next working day.
What if an item is damaged or something is missed?
Tell us as soon as you notice — ideally within 24 hours of delivery — with your booking reference and a photo where possible. We'll review the order with you and agree the appropriate resolution under our cancellation and refund policy.
How do monthly plans work?
Each plan gives you a set number of clothing items, bedsheets and duvets per month, with pickup and delivery included. Unused item counts roll over as described on the plan. Our team activates the plan and confirms your first pickup.

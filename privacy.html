Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

DIEMEZ CLEANS ("we", "us") provides laundry, cleaning, painting and interior finishing services in Bayelsa State, Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address, pickup or service address.
  • Order information: the items or services requested, pickup and delivery details, notes you add.
  • Account information: a password (stored in hashed form on our servers), your order history and, where enabled, your referral code.
  • Payment records: the amount, method, date and reference of payments. We do not store card numbers or banking credentials on this website.
  • Messages you send us through the contact or quote forms.

How we use it

  • To confirm, carry out and deliver your orders and appointments.
  • To contact you about a booking, a quotation or a change to your order.
  • To keep records of payments, invoices and any refunds or cancellations.
  • To maintain your account, saved address and order history.
  • To improve our services and understand which services customers request.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to deliver a service — for example with our own operational staff, or with a payment provider when you pay electronically. We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so.

Where your data is stored

Order and account records are stored in a private Google Sheets database accessed through our own Google Apps Script backend. Access is limited to authorised DIEMEZ CLEANS staff accounts. The website itself is served as static files and does not store customer records in the browser beyond what is needed to keep you signed in.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed to operate the business and meet accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your account details at any time, subject to records we must legally retain.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to stop sending service messages, though we may still contact you about active orders.
  • You can use the site without creating an account by booking as a guest, providing only the details needed for that order.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, access to the staff dashboard requires an administrator login, and payment confirmations are recorded by our team rather than trusted from the browser. No system is completely secure, so please use a unique password and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and, before the live backend is connected, to store demo data on your own device. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
